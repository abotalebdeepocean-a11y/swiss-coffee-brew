## Product profile additions (Product.tsx + landing)
### BAR INTENSO — Master Profile
- Blend: 30% Arabica Guatemala SHB 1670m (washed, volcanic soil) + 70% Robusta Colombia Premium (natural/honey)
- Roast: Post-roast blended — Arabica medium to 2nd crack, Robusta medium-dark inside 2nd crack; Agtron 45-50
- Sensory (10=high): Body 9/10 | Crema 9/10 with dark tiger flecking, 90s+ hold | Aroma 9/10 (dark chocolate, smoky, black spice, oak) | Sweetness 6/10 (burnt caramel, sugar molasses) | Acidity 3/10 (low, milk-safe) | Bitterness 8/10 (noble, dark chocolate 85%) | Aftertaste 9/10 (long, chocolate & sweet tobacco)
- Tasting: Dark chocolate • Spicy black pepper • Smoky • Walnut • Caramelized sugar
- Milk performance: Built for milk — low acid never curdles milk; high body + noble bitterness penetrates milk and stays. Excels in Cortado/Piccolo, Flat White (Nutella texture), Cappuccino (classic 3-layer), large Latte up to 300ml, Iced Latte (dark chocolate → cold Nutella).
- Dial-in: Espresso 18.5-19g → 36-38g (1:2), 26-29s @ 92-93°C, fine grind (one notch finer than premium). Moka: medium-fine, low heat, stop before sputtering. Not recommended for V60 (too intense). AeroPress ok: 17g, 200ml, 2min, inverted.
- Freshness: Roasted daily, packed same day, delivered within 72h. Peak 7-10 days post-roast (robusta degassing), excellent to 30 days. Store cool/dry/dark, reseal tight; consume within 4 weeks of opening.

### PREMIUM ROVENTO — Master Profile
- Blend: 100% Arabica, Colombia + Guatemala, carefully selected
- Roast: Medium roast
- Sensory: Rich crema, smooth body, balanced, refined, layered sweetness
- Milk performance: Smooth with milk (latte, cappuccino), balanced sweetness shines
- Dial-in: Espresso medium-fine, standard dose; pairs with V60/filter for clarity
- Freshness: Same daily-roast promise
